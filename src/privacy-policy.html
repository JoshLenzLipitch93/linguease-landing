Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026. Version 2.

  • Linguease is run by one person in Ireland. Your account, your words and your progress are stored with Google Firebase so they follow you between devices.
  • We use app analytics (Mixpanel and Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports to see what works and fix what doesn't.
  • App versions up to 1.3.1 include Meta's ad measurement software, which can track you only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. It's removed from version 1.3.2, which doesn't track you at all.
  • Payments go through Apple. We never see your card.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads in the app.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings → Delete account). Email info@lingueaseapp.com for anything else.

Who we are

Linguease is an app and website run by Josh Lenz-Lipitch, a sole trader based in Ireland, trading as Linguease. In this policy "we" and "us" means Linguease. We decide how your information is used, which makes us the "controller" under data protection law.

Contact: info@lingueaseapp.com for privacy questions and requests, support@lingueaseapp.com for help with the app.

This policy covers the Linguease iOS app and its widgets, and the website lingueaseapp.com.

What we collect and why

Each item below says what we collect, why, our legal reason for using it (the "legal basis" the GDPR asks for) and who stores it for us.

Your account

Your email address and password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it), or the details Apple or Google give us when you sign in with them: usually your name, your email address (with Apple this can be a private relay address) and an account ID. We also give your account an ID of its own.

Why: so your words and subscription work on all your devices. Legal basis: it's needed to provide the app you signed up for (contract). Stored by: Google Firebase Authentication.

Your words and progress

The words you save, their translations, whether you're still learning them, when you added and reviewed them, your streak, your language pair and the settings that follow your account.

Why: this is the app. Legal basis: contract. Stored by: Google Cloud Firestore.

Translations and pronunciation

When you add a word, the text you typed and your two languages are sent to Google Cloud Translation to translate it. The word and the language names are also sent to OpenAI to write out how to say it. From version 1.3.2 you can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → Pronunciation by OpenAI. Only the word and the language names are sent. Your name, email and account ID are not. Pronunciations are saved in a shared list so the next person adding the same word gets the answer straight away; that list isn't linked to anyone. Some translations happen on your phone with Google ML Kit, and that text doesn't leave your device.

Translations and pronunciations are produced by AI and can be wrong.

Why: to translate and pronounce new words. Legal basis: contract (it's part of the app you signed up for). Processed by: Google (Cloud Translation, ML Kit) and OpenAI.

Speaking practice

If you choose to say a word out loud, iOS asks for the microphone and speech recognition. Your recording is sent to Apple's speech recognition service to turn it into text. We never receive or keep the audio.

Legal basis: your permission, which you can turn off in iOS Settings at any time. Processed by: Apple.

Your subscription

Apple takes payment and handles billing, refunds and cancellation. We never see your card or billing address. RevenueCat tells us whether you have Premium, which product you're on, and when your trial or subscription started and ends. To do this RevenueCat also receives your account ID, the country of your App Store account, your IP address and an app-specific device ID.

Why: to unlock Premium. Legal basis: contract. Processed by: RevenueCat; Apple acts on its own terms as the seller.

How the app is used (analytics)

We record what happens in the app: which screens you open, onboarding steps, words added, when the paywall is shown, and app opens. Each event comes with your account ID, your device model, iOS and app version, and a rough location (country or city) worked out from your IP address. RevenueCat also adds your subscription status and purchases to that profile, so we can see which features lead people to subscribe. Your analytics profile is keyed on your account ID, not your name or email. App versions up to 1.3.1 also added your email address and name to your Mixpanel profile; from version 1.3.2 they are removed from it the next time you open the app.

Why: to see where people get stuck and what to improve. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app. From version 1.3.2 you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics. In any version you can email us and we'll delete your analytics profile. Processed by: Mixpanel and Google Analytics for Firebase.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a report with the technical details, your device model, iOS and app version, and your account ID is sent to us.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in fixing bugs. Processed by: Google Firebase Crashlytics.

Keeping the service safe

The app proves to our servers that it's a genuine copy (Firebase App Check with Apple's DeviceCheck), and fetches settings that let us switch features on or off without a new release (Firebase Remote Config). We count how many translations and pronunciations each account uses per day to keep usage fair. If you use the app without signing in, we count per network instead, using a scrambled version of your IP address rather than the address itself.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in preventing abuse. Processed by: Google Firebase; Apple.

Ad measurement (Meta, versions up to 1.3.1)

App versions up to 1.3.1 include Meta's software so we can tell which Instagram and Facebook ads lead to installs. It sends Meta an event when the app is installed and opened, with technical details about your device and app and a Meta ID created for this app. If you allowed tracking when iOS asked, Meta also receives your device's advertising identifier. Until 24 November 2026 RevenueCat also sent Meta an event when someone started a trial or subscribed.

From version 1.3.2 the app no longer includes Meta's software and no longer asks to track you, and the RevenueCat connection to Meta is switched off for everyone. If you're still on an older version, updating stops it; you can also turn tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Legal basis: your permission for the advertising identifier; our legitimate interest in measuring our own advertising for the basic install events. Received by: Meta Platforms, under its own privacy policy.

Feedback questions

Now and then we ask subscribers a short question, in the app or by email, for example why they switched off renewal or how they would feel if they could no longer use Linguease. Answering is optional and you can close the question at any time. If you answer, we keep the question, when it was asked, the option you chose, anything you choose to type, and whether you would like a call. This is stored with your account so we don't ask too often: at most once every 30 days, and not again for 90 days after you answer. Email addresses and phone numbers you type are removed automatically before we read your answer. Our app analytics record that a question was shown and which option you tapped, never what you typed. We use your answers only to improve Linguease and, if you asked for a call, to email you to arrange one. We don't use them for advertising and we never sell them.

Please don't include health details or other sensitive information in your answers.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app. You can object at any time by emailing info@lingueaseapp.com, and we will stop asking you. Stored by: Google Firebase (where your account lives) and Airtable, our internal workspace, where we read the answers. Email questions are sent from Gmail, and the answer page runs on Cloudflare.

Messages you send us

If you use "Send feedback" in the app, we receive your message, the app version, your account email and account ID. If you email us, we receive whatever you write and your email address.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering you and fixing problems. Stored by: Airtable (in-app feedback) and Gmail (email, forwarded by our domain provider Porkbun).

Our website

lingueaseapp.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, page requested) for a short time to run the site. When you tap one of our short links (for example lingueaseapp.com/ig), Cloudflare records the time, which link, the country and where the tap came from, but not your IP address.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in running the site and knowing which links people use. Processed by: Netlify and Cloudflare.

Kept only on your phone

The widget's words, your notification times, the apps you've chosen to show a word before opening, and your onboarding progress stay on your device. Notifications are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • From version 1.3.2 the app doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites and doesn't ask iOS for tracking permission.
  • We don't show ads in the app.
  • We don't collect your precise location, contacts or photos.
  • We don't send marketing emails or newsletters.
  • We don't use your words, answers or messages to train AI models.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with the companies that help us run Linguease. Each one is listed above next to what it receives. Most act only on our instructions under a data processing agreement ("processors"). Apple (payments, sign-in, speech), Google when you sign in with Google, and Meta use your information under their own terms as independent controllers.

  • Google: Firebase (accounts, database, analytics, crash reports, app checks, settings), Cloud Translation, ML Kit, Sign in with Google, Gmail.
  • OpenAI: pronunciations.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status.
  • Mixpanel: app analytics.
  • Meta Platforms: ad measurement in app versions up to 1.3.1.
  • Airtable: reading feedback and keeping our customer list (subscription history, no names or emails).
  • Cloudflare and Netlify: the website, short links and the feedback answer page.
  • Apple: payments, Sign in with Apple, speech recognition, DeviceCheck.
  • Porkbun: forwards email sent to our address.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to a buyer if Linguease is ever sold, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information.

Sending data outside the EU

Most of the companies above are based in the United States, and our database is stored in Google's US region. When your information goes outside the European Economic Area or the UK, it is protected in one of two ways:

  • EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension): Google, Mixpanel, Cloudflare and Netlify are certified. Meta Platforms is certified for EU data; for UK data it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (with the UK Addendum) in the company's data processing terms: RevenueCat, Airtable and OpenAI.

Apple, and Google when you sign in with Google, handle transfers under their own terms. You can check a company's certification at dataprivacyframework.gov, or ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Account, words and progress: until you delete them or your account. Our database backups are kept for 7 days, so deleted data is gone from backups within a week.
  • Subscription records: as long as your account exists. If you delete your account and want your RevenueCat record deleted too, email us. Apple keeps its own purchase records, and we keep business records where tax or accounting law requires it.
  • Analytics: your Mixpanel profile is deleted when you delete your account. Event history is kept for the period set in each analytics tool.
  • Crash reports: about 90 days.
  • Feedback answers: anything you type in answer to a feedback question is deleted after 24 months. If you delete your account, your answers are unlinked from you and anything you typed is deleted; we keep only an anonymous count of which options were chosen.
  • Emails and in-app feedback messages: as long as we need them to deal with what you asked.
  • Website logs: a short time, as set by Netlify.

Deleting your account

In the app go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in, your words, your progress and your feedback answers from our database, deletes your Mixpanel profile, disconnects Google sign-in if you used it (from version 1.3.2 also Sign in with Apple, after you confirm with Apple), and clears Linguease data from your phone. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: Apple bills it, so cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you want anything else removed, such as your subscription record at RevenueCat, email us.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you,
  • correct it,
  • delete it,
  • stop or limit using it, including objecting to analytics (from version 1.3.2 also a switch in Settings → Privacy) or feedback questions,
  • send it to you in a format you can move elsewhere,
  • withdraw a permission you gave (it doesn't affect what happened before).

Email info@lingueaseapp.com from the address on your account, or tell us your account email. We reply within one month. It's free. If we can't confirm the request is yours, we'll ask for something to check it.

Complaints. Please tell us first and we'll try to put it right; we acknowledge complaints within 30 days. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Ireland the Data Protection Commission, in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office, in Brazil the ANPD, or the authority where you live.

Brazil. The contact for your rights under the LGPD is info@lingueaseapp.com.

United States. We don't sell personal information. Our website doesn't track you, so a Do Not Track signal changes nothing. In the app, the iOS tracking setting controls the Meta measurement in versions up to 1.3.1; from version 1.3.2 there is none.

Children

Linguease isn't meant for children under 13, or under 16 in the EU and UK. We don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Your data is sent encrypted, our database only lets each account read its own data, and our servers only accept requests from genuine copies of the app. No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we'll tell the Data Protection Commission and, where the law requires it, you.

When we change this policy we update the date and version at the top and note the change below. If a change matters (a new use of your data or a new company receiving it), we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
